## World survey (hub and all six Rift maps; details, props, layout)
Method: in Play a survey camera bound after the game camera, one high shot (about 150 studs up) and one low shot (about 14 studs up) per place, hero Level 60, depth 1 of each region. Judged by eye, nothing changed yet. Studio served main at 203a55b (includes the Crystal Caves/Sky Islands palette from #186).

Across all Rift maps:
- Layout: every Rift is the same shape, a single narrow straight chain of rooms (walled box, short lane, next box) with the portal at the start. No branching, no loops, no side rooms, no landmarks you could navigate by; the maps differ only in colours and props.
- Outside the corridor is a huge flat ground plane (the "lawn", sand, cloud or void) ringed by oversized, repetitive rock/crystal blocks. The playable strip is tiny next to it, and the ring is the same blocky shape in every region.
- Props sit almost only along the walls; the room interiors are open floor with a few enemies. Walls are plain tiled boxes.

Per place:
- Hub "Lumenhaven" (best map): walled square town, star plaza with fountain, red-roofed houses, market stalls, lamps, farm strips, corner towers, a beam to the Rift gate, floating islands in the sky. Problems: the town is small on a very large flat bright-green lawn (a big empty ring outside the walls); the backdrop is a ring of chaotic oversized white/blue rock blocks and waterfalls that clash with the tidy town; no paths or props outside the walls; the Great Build board floats very large over the plaza from some angles.
- Green Plains: neon-green flat floor with no ground detail (no grass variation, flowers, paths), plain grey stone walls, sparse trees/shrubs; looks like an empty test arena next to the hub.
- Crystal Caves: after #186 readable, but dark navy with too many identical cyan hexagon crystals on the horizon; the corridor floor is a plain tile, almost no stalagmites/pools/ruins inside the rooms; sky and walls blend.
- Mushroom Swamp: the giant mushrooms are a good prop, but the palette reads purple/blue, not a green bog; the thick violet fog hides the floor, and the swamp water and bog props are barely visible.
- Fire Desert (best Rift map): layered mesa strata, smoking lava vents, dark red walls against a sand floor, clear silhouettes. Could use more varied rocks/bones/ruins in the rooms.
- Sky Islands: a floating checker path in a cloud sea with distant islands and a castle; the path has no walls, rails or ruins, the stone reads grey-blue, so the corridor looks like a bare tile strip.
- Altered World: purple void with neon hexagon crystals and floating cubes, readable; the horizon hexagons are the same as Crystal Caves in a different colour, and the floor is a plain dark checker.

Suggested order (owner decides): 1. layout: give the Rift corridor some shape (side rooms, a wider arena before the boss, a bend or two) in a shared generator, 2. outer ring: replace the repeated rock-block ring with a per-region horizon (mountains for Plains, cave ceiling for Caves, dunes for Desert, etc.) and trim the flat ground, 3. props per region (room interiors, ground detail, wall variety), 4. hub polish (outside the walls, rock backdrop, Great Build board scale).

## Caveats
- The Studio MCP sends synthetic mouse moves with `Delta = 0`; drag tests need many small `moveTo` steps and cursor-based panning.
- `screen_capture` shows GUI reliably, 3D is often dark.
- Rojo only syncs scripts in Edit mode; editing a script in Studio can stop it applying disk changes.
- The test place equals the published main build unless Rojo is connected.
