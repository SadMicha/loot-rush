## Game description (915 of 1,000 characters)

Smash, loot, level up: a brick-built action RPG where every hit pops studs!

Pick one of four heroes - Knight, Ranger, Spark Mage or Mushroom Shaman - and fight through Rift Trails full of swarming enemies, elites and big bosses. Dodge the red warnings, break the boss's guard and unleash 8 skills per class.

Every kill can drop gear. Hunt Rares, Epics and Uniques, forge them stronger, and build your hero with a 100-node Star Web and spell stones. Fill the Rush meter for a golden burst of speed.

A grey force is smoothing the world flat. Clear regions to bring their studs back, climb World Tiers and push your best depth up the leaderboards.

- 4 classes, up to 12 heroes per account
- Fast runs, great on phone, PC and tablet
- Bosses with phases you can learn and beat
- Pets, cosmetics, auras and trails
- Daily rewards and a Beta Season with free rewards

Play now and tell us what to improve in the Beta!
